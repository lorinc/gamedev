# Shape exploration: which game goes around the toy

Started 2026-10-02. A working folder for one exercise: find the shape (mechanics and loops) to build around the cave
ecosystem simulation, matched to an audience that will be thrilled by it. Nothing here is decided unless it says so.
Quotes are the user's, with spelling lightly fixed.

## Status (2026-10-02)

- Stages 1 to 3 are done: 29 shapes in the pool, split into variants, consolidated into two anchors.
- Stage 4 is half done: the motivation mix and its synergy are mapped (Claude's inference). The market part
  (competition, success rate, narrowly scoped indies) is not started.
- Stage 5 is parked until the end.

## Ground rules the user set

- The guides in `guides/game-design/` (04, 06, 07, 08, 09, and 10 in full) are relevant, and are **not hard limits**.
  "We are in an exploration phase, I'm trying to match my preferences, my skills, my vision to an audience who will be
  thrilled by it, and this can not be exclusively 'ME'-driven, that's just one part of the whole."
- Factors that the audience's expectations determine are **not decided out of thin air**. They are listed in
  [audience-factors.md](audience-factors.md) and stay TBD until the audience is chosen.
- Stage 1 widens the pool of candidate shapes for the market assessment to judge. It does not ask the user what they
  personally want, and it does not design the inside of any one shape.
- Questions are open. A question that comes with a menu of answers is leading.
