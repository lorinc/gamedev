# Factors the audience decides

Supporting document for the [shape exploration](README.md). All values are **TBD until the audience is chosen.**

The user's rule: these are set by what the chosen audience expects, not by taste or by guessing. The user named the
first, the third and the fourth; the rest are taken from the user's definition of an audience
([strategy/09](../../guides/strategy/09-audience-and-game-shape.md): "the same mechanics, depth, pressure-and-release
cadence and art style") and from the guides. The one Claude added is marked.

| # | Factor | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How long the core (primary) loop lasts | user; [06](../../guides/game-design/06-gameplay-loops.md) | TBD |
| 2 | How long the medium (secondary) loop lasts, and what limit forces it to turn | 06; parked by the user for stage 5 | TBD |
| 3 | The unit of play: how long a sitting is, and what is left when it ends (a run that resets, a place that persists, a level finished) | user; 06 | TBD |
| 4 | The number of viable options at a given moment, and how far it swings between playing the loadout and designing it | user; [10](../../guides/game-design/10-depth-vs-complexity.md) | TBD |
| 5 | How much the player must learn before the fun starts, and how fast new systems arrive | 10 | TBD |
| 6 | How strong the pressure is, and the cadence of pressure and release | user's audience definition; [07](../../guides/game-design/07-keeping-players-engaged.md) | TBD (bounded above by "peaceful") |
| 7 | How much content variety the players of that genre expect | [09](../../guides/game-design/09-repetition-and-variety.md) | TBD |

## Notes that came up during the stages

- **Factor 3:** if creature requests become a random progress ladder ([02-deepen.md](02-deepen.md)), how often a player
  gets a fresh ladder (once per save, or once per run) is this factor.
- **Factors 1 and 2:** the user's worry that the expedition "would make the gameplay loop too big" belongs here.
- **Factor 6:** difficulty decides whether runs, the card hand, orders and the front reach the Challenge motivation at
  all ([04-assess.md](04-assess.md)).
- **Factor 9:** answered by the user for one variant only. For the deadline, a missed attempt costs only the wait:
  "If not, can try again later."
