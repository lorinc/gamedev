# Spelunking · decision ledger

Append-only. A decision is never edited or deleted. A new decision supersedes it, and the old row's status becomes `superseded by Dnnn`. **From** is the timeline entry that produced the decision, or `—` for decisions made in design sessions outside a prototype. The design itself lives in the [concept doc](../../concepts/spelunking_base.md). This ledger is how its decisions changed.

D001–D023 were reconstructed on 2026-09-25 from the session notes.

| D038 | 2026-09-25 | p4 | active | The backpack (user's design): 6 slots of 16 units, drawn as a rectangle on the character's back, 2 slots wide by 3 tall. | D039 | 2026-09-25 | p4 | active | Stairs are planks (refines D029; the user's stair, named "plank" by the user, the tread model proposed by Claude). A plank is a tread along the top of an open cell: you stand on it from the cell above, or walk through its cell under it. Nothing digs it, since moves pass through. Falls land on it, and it isn't a wall to cling to. Every build places a plank (user's call: all planks for now); solid Built stays for older rulesets. It fixes the tick-6659 crossing: ↗ lays a plank at the top of the ↘ tunnel, and the ↘ tunnel stays open under it (example `plank-crossing`). | D046 | 2026-09-25 | p4 | active | Not built yet (next session). Flick and hold (user's design; the timing and input details proposed by Claude and accepted). A **flick** follows the world: it walks the terrain, tunnels into walls, and walks up and down steps and ramps. It never builds and never digs a step you could walk up; where it would have to, the run stops ("?"). A **hold** draws a straight line: it keeps the swipe's exact direction and height, lays a plank where the floor is missing (a 1-deep ramp mouth too) and digs a step in the way. It takes a step, pauses 0.3 s, and stops when released, always after a whole step. Nothing asks for confirmation any more. Building is a high-value precision action you don't do often, so holding feels like control, not a chore (user). A hold is a finger still down 0.3 s after the swipe registers. Walking and tunnelling start at once; a building or step-digging step waits for that moment, so the shortest hold is exactly one step. Hold+swipe is the same intent as swipe+hold. The teleport long press goes from 0.7 s to 1 s. Keyboard: tap = flick, held = hold, for every key (WASD no longer walks while held). The controls are unusual, on PC especially, so they need a tutorial (TODO). From a screenshot: there was no way to bridge over the mouth of a down-ramp. When built, this supersedes D041 and D044. |
